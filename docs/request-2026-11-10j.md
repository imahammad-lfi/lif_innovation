# Request 2026-11-10 (j) — product hero: transparent on-dark logo instead of the white plate

User: on product pages (e.g. https://lfinew.lfidemo.com/products-and-accelerators/linkworks), instead of the
logo on a white plate, show a transparent logo (or text). Options were compared; the user chose option 1, a
transparent "on-dark" logo with a text fallback.

## Why new artwork
The originals are drawn for white backgrounds: Linkworks, testoriumZ, VajraX, VectorX and AI Governance have
black or navy lettering, which disappears on the dark hero photo. textIQ, FinSight and EduPilot use a dark
royal blue that reads poorly on dark.

## Changes
- `tools/mk_ondark_logos.mjs` (new; sharp, resolved through next): `logos/<slug>.png` →
  `logos/on-dark/<slug>-on-dark.png`. Rules per pixel, alpha kept: black/navy (l < 0.25) and grey anti-aliasing
  (s < 0.2, l < 0.8) become white; dark royal blue (hue 205–245, s > 0.45, l < 0.55) becomes the same hue at
  l = 0.68; orange, yellow and bright-blue accents are unchanged. All 8 were reviewed on a dark preview. New
  file names, because Strapi media are matched by file name. Linkworks stays slightly jagged because its source is
  only 504×78; ask the brand owner for official dark-background artwork.
- `seed/custom/apply.py` `patch_product_hero`: the hero logo is `custom/logos/on-dark/<slug>-on-dark.png` when it
  exists, otherwise the text title. Menu and tile logos still use the originals.
- `page-hero.tsx`: no white plate; the logo sits on the photo (48/58/68/80 px high, max width 340–560 px) with
  `drop-shadow(0 2px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.55))`. The h1 keeps the sr-only title.

## Rollout and checks
- apply.py: only the 8 product page files changed. seed:validate: 84 documents valid.
- CMS check: all pages had been re-imported at 20:07–20:08 UTC by another seed run. A text comparison with
  seed/data showed only alt-text and enum-default differences, so there were no editor edits. Seed
  `--only=pages`: 71 updated, 8 media uploaded.
- Build, standalone static/public copy, pm2 restart. The served pages kept the old logo until the content cache
  was flushed (`POST /api/revalidate?now=1` with the secret header): the seed-run webhook does not reach the site
  reliably (see 10e). Afterwards all 8 heroes serve `*_on_dark_*.png`.
- Screenshots (Playwright, 1440×900 and 390×844): Linkworks, textIQ, VajraX, AI Governance and FinSight are legible
  on the photo; the text fallback is unchanged for the 5 products without a logo.
