## Completed implementation

Only the six assigned page JSON files were created or edited. Retired documents were read as references, not modified. The IEP hub, shared code, seed/data source, navigation and redirects were not edited directly. Publication used tools/publish_page.py with explicit owned filenames only.

Every page follows the sub-service template, with 8–9 sections, British English, short outcome-led copy, matching sticky tabs, blue-accent tiles, alternating navy/ink tile tones, existing approved media, platform/product logos on white plates, contextual SEO and one contact form last. All social strips, detail-page insights, unsupported stats, generic banners and duplicate capability/process blocks are gone. No corporate savings/speed metrics were substituted for platform results. Modernisation & programme assurance, Linkworks and delivery-accelerator routes use the final URL map.

Missing proof was replaced with capability-led artefacts and explicitly illustrative engagement patterns, not invented client cases, deployment claims or operational guarantees. The exact application/module, edition/deployment, integration and local requirements are agreed during discovery. All credentials beyond the supported source evidence were withdrawn rather than promoted into a new proof band.

## SAP source consolidation decisions

- **SAP landing page:** five-way legacy link grid removed; one SAP S/4HANA implementation, migration, integration, data, adoption and support scope created. “Latest release”, employee-values copy and the corporate-style statistics wall removed.
- **Core SAP expertise:** acquisition consolidation/harmonisation, divestiture/data separation, shared-service realignment and template-led global/local rollouts retained with inputs, outputs and controls. Universal team experience and the unapproved five-month versus 9–12-month manufacturing claim removed.
- **SAP HANA:** fit-gap, conversion versus new implementation, custom-code/integration/data/cutover choices retained on SAP; the detailed cross-platform lifecycle sits on AMS. Database/Basis, sizing, backup/recovery, availability and security are separately scoped. Appliance, virtualisation and data-centre choices are not default requirements. HANA Live, automatic performance uplift and pioneering Activate claims removed.
- **SAP special expertise:** Fiori role/task adoption retained. EHS, commerce-to-ERP and supply-chain planning appear only as explicitly scoped specialist questions pending technical confirmation; no copied EHS/Hybris text, Model Company promotion or unsupported product coverage retained.
- **SAP Data Services:** source profiling, mappings/cleansing, test loads, reconciliation/exception reports and controlled cutover folded into AMS. General data-platform needs link to Data & AI. No unnamed “our intuitive interface”, any-source integration or effortless compliance claim retained.
- **Services and More:** release/change/regression and deployment-specific technical operations retained as lifecycle responsibilities. All advertising, promotions, brand activation, consumer-behaviour and marketing-agency content removed, not transferred elsewhere.
- **ERP hub:** no duplicate replacement hub created. Redirect to the portfolio owner’s IEP hub, where the complete platform directory belongs.
