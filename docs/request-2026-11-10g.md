# Request 2026-11-10 (g) — mega menu: visible scrollbar for tall panels

User: "for services etc. some panels when I open or hover it, we need a scroll bar to see all items or cards in
that panel."

## Root cause (apps/web/src/components/layout/header-client.tsx, MegaMenu)
- The panel content already scrolled (`overflow-y-auto`), but `[scrollbar-width:none]` hid the scrollbar, so
  nothing showed that there were more cards.
- Height cap `100vh − header − 64 px strip`: on 1366×768 and smaller laptop screens, Services and Products
  overflow. Between 1024 and 1280 px wide the layout is a single column, so it almost always overflows.
- No "more below" cue, and the wheel spilled over to the page at the panel's end.

## Changes (source only)
- globals.css: new `@utility menu-scroll`, a thin scrollbar with an orange `--color-brand` thumb on a
  `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` track (`scrollbar-width`/`scrollbar-color`), plus a `::-webkit-scrollbar` fallback for
  browsers without `scrollbar-color` (8 px, rounded thumb). User chose orange.
- header-client.tsx MegaMenu:
  - New full-width scroller (`menu-scroll`, same max heights as before, `overscroll-contain`) wrapping the
    centred grid, so the scrollbar sits at the right edge of the screen rather than inside the 1920 px column.
  - A bottom fade (56 px, transparent → #070a14) shows while more content is below and fades out at the end.
    It is measured on scroll and with a ResizeObserver (images loading, window resize), and scroll position
    resets when switching menus.
- Cards, the 64 px backdrop strip, and the hover/click behaviour from 2026-11-10f are unchanged.

## Checks
- `pnpm exec eslint src/components/layout/header-client.tsx` and `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`: clean.
- Browser QA not run here (no Playwright or Chromium). Manual check needed at 1366×768 and 1024–1280 px wide:
  orange bar visible on Services and Products, fade disappears at the bottom, page does not scroll behind.
