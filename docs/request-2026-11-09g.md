## Badge / trademark compliance — sources and decisions

- **Great Place To Work** — Certification & Lists Terms (https://www.greatplacetowork.com/certification-best-workplaces-lists-terms,
  dated 1 Oct 2026), §5: "Customer may use and display the Certification Badge only during the Certification Period";
  an expired badge may be shown only if a new current badge has been received and only to show continuous history.
  Certification Period = 12 months. Badge on record reads "Certified SEP 2023 – SEP 2024 INDIA" → expired.
  **Decision:** removed from the footer and hero credentials strip; Careers tile keeps a dated text reference
  (historical, India, Sep 2023 – Sep 2024) without the badge. Restore when a current badge is supplied.
- **FT / Statista Africa's Fastest-Growing Companies 2026** — https://rankings.statista.com/en/fastest-growing-companies/rankings/fastest-growing-companies-africa-2026/
  FAQ "How can I get a license to showcase my award?" → award logo licensing options via the award portal
  (licensing packages incl. certificate/trophy). 129 companies ranked; revenue growth 2021–2024.
  **Decision:** official logo artwork not displayed unless Linkfields confirms a Statista logo licence. Replaced by
  our own text badge `custom/badges/growth-ranking-2026.png` and logo-free news card
  `custom/badges/news-growth-ranking-2026.jpg`; factual text reference to the ranking and publishers retained.
  Supplied artwork kept at `custom/badges/ft-africa-fastest-growing-2026.png` for re-use once licensed.
- **ISO** — https://www.iso.org/certification.html: "ISO does not perform certification … and it does not permit
  anyone to use the ISO logo in connection with certification"; "If you wish to use a logo to demonstrate
  certification, contact the certification body that issued the certificate."
  The ISO 9001 footer badge (`s3/Iso Logo (1) (1).png`) contains ISO's globe logo → replaced by neutral
  `custom/badges/iso-9001.png` (same family as the 27001 badge, text only). Best practice: use the certification
  body's own marks for both certificates.
- Keep, conditional on active status: D-U-N-S Registered seal (D&B subscription), NASSCOM member, YES member,
  B-BBEE Level 1 badge incl. Proudly South African mark (membership).
- OEM logos (SAP, Microsoft/Azure, Salesforce, Oracle, HP, Odoo, Zoho, UiPath, Blue Prism): trademarks used
  nominatively for genuine partnerships; recommended: swap corporate logos for each vendor's partner-programme badge
  from its partner portal where available. New colour logos this round from Wikimedia Commons (logos below the
  threshold of originality / trademark-only).
- Map: Google Maps embed (attribution inside the iframe) — compliant.
