# Digital Transformation sub-services review

Keep all six required sub-service URLs, but rework them around one nine-section, outcome-led template. Cloud services (17 sections) and App modernisation (15) are legacy composites; Integration services carries a copied cloud-development process. Remove unsupported 60% and 10x claims, correct the RPA tool logos, foreground Linkworks/testoriumz and secure-by-design delivery, and replace repeated benefits with specific scenarios and evidenced results.

**Verdict:** rework all six pages; retain the required service taxonomy and URLs. This is advice only; no implementation is performed.

## Standard sub-service template

Use **nine core sections**, with service-specific content rather than a repeated corporate deck:

1. **Hero + sticky tabs:** required service H1, outcome-led subtitle, contextual CTA; Overview / What we deliver / Use cases / Our approach / Get in touch. Preserve docking and active-section behaviour; give proof/IP blocks anchors if needed without overcrowding mobile tabs.
2. **Buyer trigger + outcomes:** who this is for, why act, constraints and three measures to baseline. One compact overview, not a definition of the technology.
3. **What we deliver:** four to six unified orange-accent tiles with concrete outputs and clear service boundaries. One grid only.
4. **Typical scenario / architecture:** a labelled illustrative workflow or approved real example with systems, handovers, controls and cross-portfolio roles.
5. **Relevant IP & accelerators:** product logo on white plate, practical fit, reusable artefacts, product link and standalone licensing where applicable. Never force every product onto every page.
6. **Our approach — secure by design:** one live-text four/five-phase process, deliverables, gates, responsibilities and operating handover. Graphics support rather than contain the information.
7. **Evidence:** one approved case or reference with baseline, scope, period, IP used and actual results; no invented placeholders masquerading as proof.
8. **Engagement & buyer questions:** suitable commercial models plus four to six genuinely decision-useful answers. Link full commercial-model details to Our approach rather than copying the entire deck.
9. **Contextual contact:** service preselection, useful scoping questions, verified next steps and privacy notice. A relevant resource pair can be included just before this section only when available; no mandatory global news/social tail.

**Position convention:** numbered rows preserve the original page JSON sections array exactly. `Add before N` is an insertion slot relative to that original array, not a renumbered final layout; multiple additions at one slot are intentionally possible. Recommendations describe consolidation/reordering into the template above. Empty source headings are labelled with their rendered global fallback.
