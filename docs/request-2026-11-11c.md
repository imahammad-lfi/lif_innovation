# Request - 11 Nov 2026 10:33 - product banner logo edges

> "please see attached images and it looks edges are spreading. give a fine looking with proper ending etc.."
> (screenshots: Linkworks, testoriumz and VajraX logos on their product page banners)

## Finding
- The product logos only exist as small bitmaps (for example Linkworks 504×78, testoriumz 685×118, VajraX 315×107 px of
  artwork), cut out of a white background. The on-dark banner versions made in the previous round were PNGs derived
  from them. On the banner they are drawn at up to 400 CSS px wide, so on 2x-3x screens they were enlarged 1.5-3x and
  re-compressed by the Next.js image optimiser. That produced exactly what the screenshots show: soft, lumpy edges,
  stair-steps on curves and a pale fringe (the remains of the white background in the edge pixels).
- No SVG or larger originals are published on the product sites (checked again this round). The testoriumz site's
  footer logo was no larger than ours.

## Fix
1. **New tool `tools/vectorise_logos.py`** traces each original logo into a clean SVG:
   - removes the white matte from the edge pixels (un-premultiplies against white), so no pale fringe remains;
   - finds the logo's flat brand colours, builds a coverage map for each colour, smooths the outline (stair-steps and
     compression bumps removed) and traces it to smooth curves with potrace;
   - writes a light-background version (`logos/svg/<slug>.svg`, original colours) and the on-dark banner version
     (`logos/svg/<slug>-on-dark.svg`: near-black/navy wordmark to white, dark brand blue lifted to a lighter tint of the
     same hue, orange and yellow unchanged, as in the previous round);
   - the SVG frame is the artwork plus 1 px, so proportions match the old files to within about 2% and banner sizes do
     not change.
   - Flat-colour logos (Linkworks, testoriumz, VectorX, AI Governance, textIQ, FinSight, EduPilot) become pure vector,
     3-13 KB each. VajraX has a shaded 3D-style icon that cannot be flattened without losing its look, so its original
     shading (cleaned, WebP) sits inside a traced vector outline: the edges are crisp and the shading is kept (42 KB).
2. **Seed pipeline**: `deck.product_hero_logo()` now points the eight product banners at the on-dark SVGs. Regenerated
   seed data: exactly 8 changed lines (one logo per product page), nothing else.
3. **CMS**: pages imported - 8 SVGs uploaded, 71 pages updated and published. Strapi records each SVG's width and
   height (for example Linkworks 506×80), which the banner uses for its area-based sizing.
4. **Website**: no code change needed. SVGs are served directly from `/uploads/…svg` (never through the image
   optimiser), so they stay sharp at any size and density.
5. **Docs**: DESIGN-SYSTEM.md (banner logos must be vector; how the tracer works; how to judge the result) and
   PRODUCTS-AND-ACCELERATORS.md (SVG files, tool requirements, editor note: upload an SVG made for dark backgrounds,
   or a transparent PNG at least about 800 px wide).
