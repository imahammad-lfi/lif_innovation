# Legacy sub-industry pages — consolidate into two stronger industry destinations

## Summary

**Merge all five pages.** Banking, Insurance and Fintech should become substantial, directly addressable sector sections within **BFSI**; Mining and Oil & Gas should become sector sections within **Energy & Mining**. Preserve those sector names in navigation, not five additional near-duplicate landing pages. Implement permanent redirects only after the destination sections exist. This recommendation preserves the client’s eight-industry taxonomy and five fixed portfolios.

The newer umbrella copy already absorbs almost all useful legacy topics. The children add stock imagery, duplicated service cards and unsupported promises, rather than evidence of specialist delivery. The most urgent corrections are the Banking and Insurance meta descriptions, which falsely describe Linkfields as a bank and insurer; Mining’s patronising “non-IT industries” heading; and claims of guaranteed compliance, fraud prevention, personalised financial advice and higher profits.

**Evidence and scope:** Read the complete review brief, corporate deck, all five page JSONs, both umbrella JSONs and relevant global defaults. ## Group-level findings and implementation handoffs

### P1 — Consolidate without losing sector findability

The umbrella **“Specialist focus across financial services”** already links to these BFSI children; **“Focused expertise across resource operations”** links to Mining and Oil & Gas. Change those cards and the header’s sector chips to the **new parent-sector anchors**, not redirecting child links. Keep the dark eight-industry photo-card grid, including Public Sector and Education; keep the five fixed service portfolios. Do not flatten BFSI and Energy & Mining into a single undifferentiated industry list.

Publish the Banking, Insurance, Fintech, Mining and Oil & Gas destination modules first; verify anchor scrolling below the fixed header and mobile reachability; then permanently redirect the legacy URLs, update internal links and sitemap entries, and set canonical URLs to the two parent pages (without fragments). Preserve query parameters where useful. Record incoming traffic/search queries before migration; there is no supplied evidence of independent child-page search demand. Reconsider a standalone page only when it can offer a distinct buyer proposition, substantial approved delivery proof and demonstrable demand—not more generic copy.

### P1 — Replace unsupported assurance with controlled delivery

Do not publish **“ensure operations align with evolving regulations”**, **“detect and prevent fraud”**, **“ensuring personalized coverage”**, **“keep operations safe”**, or **“higher profits, and increased market share”** as automatic technology results. Explain human accountability, validated models, integration/data prerequisites and scope. The corporate deck establishes relevant capabilities and products, **not proof of a particular bank, insurer, fintech, mine or terminal deployment**.

### P2 — Use one outcome-led structure per umbrella

Recommended flow: **hero and overview → named sector modules with visible outcome tiles → relevant services/IP/accelerators → approved delivery evidence → engagement and contextual contact → optional curated industry resources → footer**. Use the same navy accent tile model and subtle expansion as the rest of the site. Do not rebuild all five portfolios, the corporate value-play table or the global industry grid under every sector. Explain the appropriate engagement—project, managed service or dedicated team—and link to the full commercial-model detail; keep GCC as a relevant optional scale route, not a compulsory industry claim.

The existing `building-tools` cards already look consistent with the new tile treatment; the **copy and necessity**, not another visual redesign, are the main problems. Conversely, the large `specialization-list` panel shows only one description at a time; the other descriptions are absent from the initial server-rendered page. Fintech has seven topics behind this treatment. Prefer scan-friendly visible tiles so readers and crawlers receive the complete sector proposition. Keyboard selection was verified for Banking/Insurance; do not label the whole component non-functional. Mining’s equipment hero is relevant, but its active checklist/laptop specialisation image is not. Seek real, approved diagrams/screens rather than generic AI overlays.

### P2 — Preserve, but repair, sticky section navigation

The sticky bar is an explicit client requirement: **do not remove it**. Insurance’s desktop screenshot shows the docked copy and hero copy simultaneously, with **“GET IN TOUCH”** highlighted at the top. A read-only return-to-top check reproduced two tab sets on Banking and Insurance. Hand this to the shared component owner: verify that the dock disappears when the original bar re-enters view and scroll-spy resets correctly, across smooth/fast scrolling and reduced-motion settings. Retest after consolidation; do not treat screenshot scroll state as evidence that the initial page is always broken.

- **P1, LinkedIn:** `global.json` uses `https://www.linkedin.com/company/40661516/admin/feed/posts/` in both banner and footer. Request the verified public company profile URL; do not invent the slug.
