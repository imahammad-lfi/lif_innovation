# Request 11 Nov 2026 (12:22) - VajraX logo, privacy pop-up, balanced product logos

## Request

1. Update the VajraX logo in all sections of the website where applicable (new logo supplied: transparent and white
   background versions, 2048 x 838).
2. Contact form: the Privacy Policy link does not pop up and appears to open behind the form. It should pop up, or the form
   should give way and show the privacy policy.
3. Proprietary products: all logo texts should look the same size and balanced; some currently look big and some small.

## Findings

- **VajraX** was referenced across the published content (menu links, mobile drawer, product cards on the hub, home and
  event pages, Linkfields IP tiles on service pages) plus the VajraX page banner. The new logo replaces all of them.
- **Privacy Policy link**: it was a plain link to `/privacy-policy`. Clicking it navigated the page *behind* the modal
  enquiry form, so the policy loaded out of sight and the visitor had to close the form (and lose what they typed) to read it.
- **Logo sizes**: every logo was fitted into the same plate box by its outer bounds. Logos with a tall icon (VajraX,
  FinSight, EduPilot) therefore got a small wordmark, text-only logos (VectorX, AI Governance) a large one (see the
  before screenshot in the review image).

## Changes

### 1. New VajraX logo everywhere
- `seed/assets/custom/logos/vajrax.png`: the supplied transparent logo, trimmed (1600 x 610). Old 2025 logo kept only in
  `logos/retired/vajrax-2025.png`.
- `logos/on-dark/vajrax-on-dark.png`: banner version for the dark VajraX page banner; only the black "Vajra" letters are
  turned white, the shaded icon and orange "X" are untouched. Used directly (not traced) because the new file is high
  resolution; `tools/vectorise_logos.py` now skips VajraX and the stale traced VajraX SVGs were removed.
- Imported into Strapi; the three old VajraX files (ids 994, 1133, 1141), verified unlinked, were removed from the Media
  Library so editors cannot pick the old logo by mistake.

### 2. Privacy Policy pop-up above the form
- `components/legal/privacy-dialog.tsx` (`PrivacyPolicyLink`), `components/legal/privacy-content.tsx` and
  `app/api/legal/privacy/route.ts`. The link opens a second dialog stacked above the enquiry form (z-index 1170/1180).
- Content is the published Privacy policy page (its rich-text sections), loaded when first opened, so the policy is edited
  in one place. Headings mapped to h3/h4 under the dialog title.
- Desktop: centred 760 px panel; phones: bottom sheet; own scroll area.
- Closing (x, "Back to the form", Escape, click outside) closes only the policy; the enquiry form stays open with all
  typed data, focus returns to the link. "Open the full page" opens `/privacy-policy` in a new tab; Ctrl/Cmd-click and
  middle-click keep normal link behaviour; without JavaScript the link still opens the page.
- Used by both the pop-up form and the inline form on the Contact page.

### 3. Balanced product logos
- `tools/logo_metrics.py` measures each wordmark's x-height, cap height and baseline; `tools/logo_plates.py` builds
  `logos/plate/<slug>-plate.png` for all eight products: the same transparent 3.25 : 1 canvas with the logo scaled so its
  lower-case letters are 24% of the canvas height.
- `seed/custom/apply.py` final pass swaps every product logo reference for its plate file; `apps/web/src/lib/logo-plate.ts`
  recognises plate files (`_plate_` in the file name) and the menu (124 x 38, 136 x 42 from 1920 px), drawer (112 x 34),
  product cards (`LogoChip`) and logo tiles (`LogoPlate`) use the plate shape. Partner and platform logos unchanged.
- Plate and banner logos are delivered at image quality 90.
