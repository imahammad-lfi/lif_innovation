# Talent Advisory & GCC sub-services and engagement models

**Group:** `talent-subservices` · **Reviewed:** 8 November 2026 · **Read-only review**

## Summary

Keep the four deck-mandated sub-service destinations, but merge the thin staff-augmentation and managed-teams pages into anchored sections on Specialist talent & managed teams. Keep one GCC BOT page serving both its sub-service and engagement-model roles. Replace legacy IT-support/team-administration copy, remove unsupported 10x claims, substantiate GCC footprint figures, and make deliverables, accountability, embedded IP and transfer terms concrete.

**Decision:** four canonical sub-service pages, not six thin destinations. Staff augmentation and Managed teams remain named engagement models, with detailed anchors on the specialist-talent page. GCC BOT remains one page, linked from both classifications. No change to the five client-mandated portfolios or four sub-service names.

## Evidence and method

Read the complete review brief, corporate deck, design system, all six page JSON files and relevant global fallback data. Captured every page with the approved `shot.py` command at 1440px, and Specialist talent & managed teams plus GCC BOT at 390 × 844; inspected every resulting slice. Used HTTP GET and read-only browser inspection for H1, metadata, IDs, initially collapsed process rows and mobile widths. No forms were submitted; no site files, builds, seeds or services were changed. No external sources were used.

Screenshots are under `/tmp/review/talent-subservices/`. The mobile checks found no horizontal overflow (`scrollWidth = 390`); the primary concern is content repetition and conversion depth, not a broken mobile grid. Hover/reduced-motion behaviour and form delivery were not exhaustively tested.

**Table convention:** existing `#` values are the original, 1-based JSON section positions, never renumbered. An `add` row is inserted immediately before the original section at that position. Proposed component names for additions describe content intent, not a requirement to create a new CMS component. Empty local headings inherit the quoted global fallback. **P1:** must fix; **P2:** should fix; **P3:** optional refinement.
