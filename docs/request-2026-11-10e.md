## WebKit finding — conclusion
- Headed WebKit (real X display, software rendering, no GPU): home page does render (iPad Mini 34 frames/2 s,
  Desktop Safari 9 frames/2 s); Desktop Safari profile: pop-up opens at ~10 s with opacity 1 (visible), with or
  without backdrop blur. iPad Mini emulation (DPR 2 + touch) saturates headed WebKitGTK even without blur.
- Conclusion: headless 0 fps is a test-browser artefact; real Safari composites on the GPU. Tablet/desktop pop-up
  layout is unchanged from the version already live. Recommend one real-iPad Safari spot check on the preview URL.
