## 2. Visual and interaction changes

**Partner strip (home hero).** The strip now reads SAP, Microsoft, Microsoft Azure, Salesforce, Oracle, HP, Odoo, Zoho, UiPath and Blue Prism. Each logo has two versions, a white one for the dark banner and the original colour one, which appears on a white chip on hover. The new colour logos (HP, Azure, UiPath, Blue Prism) come from Wikimedia Commons, where they are recorded as trademark-only images without copyright. While testing, we found that hovering did nothing on two-thirds of the scrolling strip: the repeated copies of the logo set had been made non-interactive. Hover and click now work on every visible logo, and keyboard users still tab through each logo once only.

**Buttons.** A hover state that only grows the button was easy to miss, so each button now also changes colour. Primary buttons go from the orange gradient to royal blue, and secondary buttons from dark outline to the orange gradient. Focus rings and the subtle lift are unchanged.

**Products menu.** Without the numbers, each tile starts directly with the product family name, so the panel is shorter and easier to scan.

**Email band (Contact page).** It now shows the heading "Prefer email? Write to the right team." next to three compact cards (Sales, General, Careers). Each card has an icon chip in a Linkfields accent colour. The heading moves above the cards below 1360 px so addresses are never cut off.

## 3. Content review: method and results

Each page and blog post was reviewed against the corporate-deck positioning, the supportive tone you asked for and the site's verified facts (offices, certifications, ranking, events). A second, stricter editorial pass then accepted, modified or rejected every suggestion. Finally, every accepted change was read by hand. Changes are applied through a single, auditable corrections file in the content pipeline, so they survive future content rebuilds.

| Stage | Count |
|---|---|
| Pages and posts reviewed | 77 |
| Findings raised | 339 (12 high, 204 medium, 123 low severity) |
| Accepted by the editorial pass | 275 |
| Rejected on manual review | 37 |
| **Corrections now live** | **242 on 73 pages and posts** |

| Type of correction | Applied | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Stronger, more specific wording | 74 | "Reimagine banking, insurance and customer services…" became "Support banking and insurance with secure digital financial services, automated workflows and connected customer experiences." |
| Missing links between related content | 38 | Terms of Use now links to the Privacy Policy. The cyber readiness solution links to Security operations, and Talent Advisory & GCC links to the office locations. |
| Supportive, non-confrontational tone | 36 | "observable pain points" became "clear improvement priorities", and "Problem and fit" became "Requirements and fit" on all four product pages. |
| Search-friendly headings | 29 | "Connect assets, data and operations" became "IT integration for *energy and mining*". |
| Consistency (names, CTAs, phrasing) | 24 | "Contact our team" became "Let’s connect", and "customer problem" became "your business requirements". |
| Repetition between sections | 17 | Industry pages that repeated the same "example engagement pattern" disclaimer now each say something specific to that industry. |
| Grammar and spelling (British English) | 14 | "Handoff" became "Hand-off", and "also referred as" became "also referred to as". |
| Flow and claims | 6 | "Modernise critical applications without losing business continuity" became "…with business continuity in mind" (no guarantee implied). |

**What we deliberately did not change.** We kept the names you specified ("Specialist talent & managed teams", the five portfolio names, product names) and the deck phrases ("One strategic partner across your transformation", "Outcomes, not offerings"). We also kept specific facts such as office lists and SAP module coverage. Rewrites that would have made copy more generic, or that would have changed a product's positioning, were rejected. No new statistics, clients or results were introduced.

**False alarms checked and closed.** Nine high-severity findings were artefacts of the review, such as "article ends mid-word" when the reviewer had seen a shortened extract. Each was verified against the live page. All Products page section links resolve, and "#38 in 2023" on the News page is backed by the FT's 2023 ranking, which is linked there.

**Duplicate sections.** The automated audit finds no section duplicated across pages; earlier rounds had already removed repeated insight cards, social banners and CTA strips. This round's 17 rewrites cleared the remaining near-duplicate passages.

## 4. SEO

- **Titles and descriptions.** All 77 pages and posts have a unique title (19–59 characters, ending "| Linkfields") and a unique description (131–158 characters). Each leads with the search phrase a buyer would use, for example "SAP S/4HANA Implementation & Migration", "Global Capability Centre Strategy & Setup" or "Banking, Insurance & Fintech IT Services".
- **Keywords.** Each page has a refreshed keyword set (628 distinct terms) built around five themes. Digital transformation and application modernisation; ERP and enterprise platforms (SAP S/4HANA, Dynamics 365, Odoo, Zoho, Salesforce); data, AI and AI governance; cyber security (exposure validation, AppSec, security operations); and Global Capability Centres, BOT and managed teams. Local intent ("… South Africa") is used where Linkfields actually operates.
- **Structured data.** The site now tells search engines that Linkfields is an organisation with six offices, gives breadcrumb trails on every page, describes each service and marks blog posts as articles. The site is declared British English, and pages without their own social image use their hero image when shared on LinkedIn, X or messaging apps.
- **Crawlability.** The sitemap and robots file are live, and unknown URLs return a true 404 to Google and Bing.
- **Further keyword opportunities** (content Linkfields would need to supply): a short case study per portfolio ("SAP S/4HANA migration case study South Africa"), a GCC guide or FAQ ("how to set up a Global Capability Centre in India"), and product demonstration pages ("AI governance platform demo"). Each targets searches that the site cannot yet answer with evidence.

## 7. Editing in the CMS

- **Partner logos:** *Content Manager → Page → Home → Hero slider → partners*. Each partner has `image` (white version for the banner), `colorImage` (original colours, shown on hover), `label` and an optional `href`. Leave `colorImage` empty to show the white logo only. The strip title is the hero's `partnersLabel` field.
- **Email band:** *Content Manager → Page → Contact us → Contact email banner*. Edit `heading` (wrap words in `*asterisks*` to highlight them in the gradient), `description` and the `contacts` list (title and email). Icons are chosen automatically from each contact's title.
- **Highlighted words in headings:** the same `*asterisks*` convention works in every section heading; `_underscores_` give the gold variant.
- **SEO:** each page and post has an **SEO** block (`metaTitle`, `metaDescription`, `keywords`, optional `ogImage` for social sharing). Keep titles under 60 characters and descriptions between 140 and 160.
