# Request 2026-11-10 (f) — desktop mega menu: hover closes on leave, click pins

User: "when we hover any top bar item … the panel opens; after coming back with the cursor the user needs to click
on it to close it." Wanted: hover opens, panel stays while the cursor is on the item or inside the panel, closes
when the cursor leaves that area; a click opens it and keeps it open, a second click closes it.

## Root cause (apps/web/src/components/layout/header-client.tsx)
1. The panel could be as tall as the viewport (`max-h-[calc(100vh-72px)]`). Large menus (Services, Products) reached
   the bottom of normal laptop screens, so the cursor never left the panel or reached the backdrop. The only ways
   out were up through the top bar or out of the window, so it felt like a click was needed.
2. Hover and click shared one open/closed state: hover opened, then a click to "keep it open" closed it
   (`isOpen ? null : label`), and a click-opened panel still closed on mouse leave. No pinned state.
3. Leaving upward crossed other nav items, which opened their panels.

## Changes (source only, no CMS or seed changes)
- header-client.tsx
  - State `{ label, path, pinned }`. Hover opens in hover mode; hovering another item switches to it in hover
    mode (option 1, approved). Leaving the item and the panel closes a hover panel after 200 ms (was 160 ms).
  - Click, Enter or Space on a nav item pins its panel (including one already open from hover); a second click
    closes it. A pinned panel ignores mouse leave.
  - Closes on: click outside the nav items and the panel (document `pointerdown`), backdrop click, Escape (focus
    returns to the nav item when focus was inside the menu), link click in the panel, route change.
  - The cursor leaving the browser window counts as leaving (hover mode only).
  - Panel max height reduced by 64 px (`calc(100vh-136px)`, 3xl `calc(100vh-148px)`), so a 64 px backdrop strip
    is always visible below the panel (approved). Longer content scrolls inside the panel.
  - Side effect: on touch screens of 1024 px and wider, a tap (mouseenter + click) now opens and pins instead of
    opening and closing straight away.
