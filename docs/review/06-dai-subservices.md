**Group:** `dai-subservices` · **Reviewed:** 8 November 2026 · **Verdict:** Rework all four pages; retain all four mandated services and URLs.

## Summary

**Data platforms & analytics is the first priority.** Its renamed hero sits above legacy Big Data Engineering copy, a grafted second overview, an unsupported “10x faster operational growth” claim and an outdated raster process. ## Group-level decisions

### P1 — Fix credibility and offer boundaries

Remove the “10x”, guaranteed ROI and guaranteed accuracy claims. The deck is not evidence for them, and a different deck metric cannot be repurposed. Distinguish governance **service**, security/GRC **service** and AI Governance **software**. Retain the five portfolios and four Data & AI sub-services exactly as requested.

Keep product pages as the **software buying/demo route** and accelerator pages as the **reusable delivery-IP route**. Sub-service pages should explain discovery, adaptation, integration, evaluation and operations around a client's problem. The relevant product/accelerator links are not a reason to reproduce every product capability here:

| Service | Primary differentiation | Relevant linked IP—not automatic inclusion |
|---|---|---|
| AI strategy & governance | Investment choices, value assumptions, ownership and operating controls | Approved Data & AI Readiness scope; AI Governance software/Cyber Security control implementation |
| Agentic AI & GenAI | Authorised knowledge, bounded tools/actions, workflow integration and release evaluation | textIQ, FinSight, EduPilot; Intelligent Workflow Automation |
| Data platforms & analytics | Sources, pipelines, platform, quality and Power BI reporting | Power BI + AI/ML; financial-context FinSight; predictive/simulation links only when relevant |
| Machine learning | Baseline, model evaluation, deployment and ongoing predictive performance | Predictive Analytics Suite; Digital Twin for simulation; Power BI + AI/ML for presentation |

The shared LinkedIn URL, `https://www.linkedin.com/company/40661516/admin/feed/posts/`, is an administrator destination. Ask the global owner to replace it with the client-approved public company URL. Do not guess a replacement.

### P2 — Standardise the specialist template without flattening the offers

Recommended sequence: **outcome-led hero/sticky tabs → buyer trigger → concrete deliverables → tailored delivery/acceptance gates → relevant service/software/IP choice → one proof example → specific CTA/contextual form**. Agentic AI can add three concise illustrative workflows; ML should merge its duplicated flow cards into process. Use one unified tile style, purple portfolio accents, approved product wordmarks on white plates and subtle hover expansion. Preserve the client-required sticky tabs.

All four pages inherit the same global tail. Although global JSON contains four insights, the renderer caps the display at **three**; there is no fourth visible RPA card on these pages. Remove repeated news from the specialist pages and consolidate social promotion into the footer. Keep and tailor the contact form. On mobile, shorten stacked repetition and show process output summaries without requiring every accordion to be opened.

Explain **integrated delivery** with actual hand-offs to application/ERP integration, Cyber Security and operational ownership—not repeated five-portfolio or eight-industry grids. Use relevant, client-confirmed commercial choices from the deck: projects, managed services, dedicated teams, etc. Do not invent assessment durations, prices, service SLAs or universal software inclusion.

### Release/UX checks—not asserted public-site failures

Each inspected route has one meaningful H1 and an appropriate title/meta description; rewrite the Data platforms description as above. Seed `canonicalUrl` values are blank and local HTML resolves canonicals to the temporary forwarded 3001 hostname. Confirm production canonicals use the intended public Linkfields domain before release; this does **not** establish a fault on the public live domain.
