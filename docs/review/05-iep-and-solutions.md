# Intelligent Enterprise Platforms and legacy Solutions review

## Decision

**P1: retire the parallel Solutions area and merge its useful platform content into Intelligent Enterprise Platforms (IEP).** Keep the client’s five portfolios unchanged. SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Odoo and Salesforce deserve distinct, reworked IEP platform pages; Zoho stays in IEP. Keep Platform migration & AMS as the shared lifecycle offer, but rebuild its SAP-heavy inherited copy into a credible cross-platform service model.

**Do not retain the five SAP sub-pages as stand-alone pages.** Core expertise has valuable acquisition/carve-out and template-rollout topics. Special expertise has potentially valuable Fiori, EHS, commerce and planning scope, subject to client confirmation. HANA has legitimate database/technical-operation content but confuses it with ERP migration. Data Services is thin and generic. Services and More is mostly irrelevant advertising-agency content and should be retired, retaining only verified lifecycle operations in AMS.

Evidence: complete review brief and 2026 corporate deck; every assigned page JSON; global navigation/defaults and design guidance; HTTP GET checks of live H1/title/section IDs; desktop full-page slices for all 12 pages and mobile slices for Zoho, AMS, ERP hub and SAP. Screenshots are in `/tmp/review/iep-and-solutions/`. No site content, build, seed or service was changed. No external searches were used; current vendor capabilities/credentials and lifecycle naming require client technical validation. Priorities: **P1** must fix; **P2** should fix; **P3** refinement. Existing positions are exact 1-based JSON section indices; added positions mean insertion at that original slot. On a retired/merged page, actions describe what survives on its destination, not work to preserve the old URL.
